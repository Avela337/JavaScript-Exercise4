My Story

From a dreamer to a luxury lifestyle entrepreneur

I've been fascinated by fashion since I was a kid, taking apart in school just to see how they it feels. What started as a hobby became a career path when I launched All Lux out of my Cape Town apartment in 2026 with nothing but a laptop, a dream, and a deep love for fashion and things.

Today, All Lux serves over 50,000 customers across South Africa, offering carefully curated fashion sense that combine cutting-edge performance with genuine value.

My mission is simple: make great fashion , affordable, and understandable for everyone — whether you're a student, a creative professional, or just someone who wants to feel luxury at great value and a little smarter.
